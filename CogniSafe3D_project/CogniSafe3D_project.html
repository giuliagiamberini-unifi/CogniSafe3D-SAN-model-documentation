Model: CogniSafe3D_model

Note on the study setup.
The model documented above (CogniSafe3D_model) is the only SAN model of the project that is described here. For simplicity, the reward variables and the whole study (range studies and performance variables) have been conducted in parallel on this same model, using two copies of it that differ only in name and in the initial marking of the scenario places:

  • CogniSafe3D_model_safeScenario: initial marking Safe_Scenario = 1 (and Dangerous_Scenario = 0). It is used to evaluate the availability of the system through the reward safe_state (reward model Reward_Availability), i.e. the steady-state probability of SAFE_STATE.
  • CogniSafe3D_model_dangerousScenario: initial marking Dangerous_Scenario = 1 (and Safe_Scenario = 0). It is used to evaluate safety through the reward catastrophic_state (reward model Reward_Safety), i.e. the steady-state probability of CATASTROPHIC_STATE.

The scenario remains the same for the whole execution, so each copy only keeps the part of the model that is reachable under its own scenario (e.g. the copies only contain the activities, gates and places relevant for that scenario). The structure, the case probabilities and the gates are otherwise identical to those of CogniSafe3D_model. The range studies and reward tables below are reported exactly as defined in the project, so they refer to the copies by their names (CogniSafe3D_model_safeScenario and CogniSafe3D_model_dangerousScenario), which are the same model as CogniSafe3D_model described above.
